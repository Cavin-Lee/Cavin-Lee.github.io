01 / ABOUT

Academic background

Wei-Kai Li is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Shandong Jianzhu University. His work develops machine learning methods and applies them to medical imaging and brain connectivity analysis.

He studied at Chongqing Jiaotong University and received his PhD from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. His research includes collaborations with medical imaging organizations.
